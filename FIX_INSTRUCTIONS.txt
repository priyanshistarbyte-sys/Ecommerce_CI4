To fix "No direct script access allowed" error:

1. Open your Apache httpd.conf file (usually in C:\xampp\apache\conf\httpd.conf)

2. Find this line and uncomment it (remove the #):
   #LoadModule rewrite_module modules/mod_rewrite.so
   
   Should become:
   LoadModule rewrite_module modules/mod_rewrite.so

3. Find all instances of "AllowOverride None" and change to "AllowOverride All"

4. Restart Apache

5. Access: http://localhost/Ecommerce_CI4/public/
